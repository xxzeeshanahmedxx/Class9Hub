## Lecture Overview
This is the Exercise lecture for Chapter 11 (Biostatistics). The teacher solves the textbook exercise questions, including MCQs, short questions, and numerical problems from the Punjab Board textbook.

## Main Concepts Explained by the Teacher
Sir goes through all exercise questions step by step. MCQs test key definitions and concepts. Short questions cover the definition and uses of biostatistics, measures of central tendency, and graphical representation of data. Numerical problems require students to calculate mean, median, and mode from given data sets, and to construct bar charts. The teacher demonstrates how to arrange data (ascending order for median), how to handle even vs. odd numbers of values, and how to choose appropriate graph scales. Exam tips include showing all calculation steps clearly to earn method marks.
