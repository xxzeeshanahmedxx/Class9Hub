# Enzymes Inhibition — Class 9 Biology Lecture Summary

## Lecture Overview
This lecture covers Enzyme Inhibition, where certain molecules interfere with enzyme activity. The teacher distinguishes between reversible and irreversible inhibition and explains how some poisons and medicines work by inhibiting enzymes.

## Main Concepts Explained by the Teacher
Sir explains two main types of inhibition. **Competitive Inhibition:** An inhibitor molecule resembles the substrate and competes for the active site. If the inhibitor binds, the substrate cannot bind. Increasing substrate concentration can overcome competitive inhibition. **Non-Competitive Inhibition:** The inhibitor binds elsewhere on the enzyme (allosteric site), changing the enzyme's shape so the active site no longer works. This cannot be overcome by adding more substrate. The teacher uses practical examples: sulfa drugs work as competitive inhibitors of bacterial enzymes, and cyanide acts as a non-competitive inhibitor of cytochrome oxidase.

## Important Definitions and Terms
- **Competitive Inhibition:** An inhibitor that competes with the substrate for the active site of the enzyme.
- **Non-Competitive Inhibition:** An inhibitor that binds to a site other than the active site, altering enzyme function.
- **Allosteric Site:** A regulatory site on an enzyme separate from the active site.
- **Inhibitor:** A substance that slows down or stops enzyme activity.

## Key Points and Lists from the Lecture
- Competitive inhibition can be overcome by increasing substrate concentration.
- Non-competitive inhibition is permanent (irreversible) in many cases.
- Many antibiotics and drugs work through enzyme inhibition.
- Poisons like cyanide and mercury are dangerous because they irreversibly inhibit vital enzymes.
- Feedback inhibition is a regulatory mechanism in metabolic pathways.

## What Students Should Remember
So basically, the difference between competitive and non-competitive inhibition is a board favorite. Remember: competitive = same seat, fight for it; non-competitive = broken lock, no key works.
