# Uses of Enzymes in Different Industries — Class 9 Biology Lecture Summary

## Lecture Overview
This practical lecture connects biology textbook knowledge to the real world. The teacher explains the commercial and industrial applications of enzymes, showing students how enzymes are used in everyday products and manufacturing processes.

## Main Concepts Explained by the Teacher
Sir discusses various industries that rely on enzymes. In the food industry, enzymes like pectinase are used to clarify fruit juices, and proteases are used to tenderize meat. In the detergent industry, lipases and proteases are added to laundry detergents to remove grease and protein stains. In the leather industry, enzymes are used to soften hides. The teacher also mentions the pharmaceutical industry, where enzymes like streptokinase are used to dissolve blood clots.

## Important Definitions and Terms
- **Pectinase:** An enzyme used to break down pectin in fruits, helping clarify juices.
- **Protease:** An enzyme that breaks down proteins, used in meat tenderizers and detergents.
- **Lipase:** An enzyme that breaks down fats and oils, used in stain removers.
- **Amylase:** An enzyme that breaks down starch, used in baking and brewing.

## Key Points and Lists from the Lecture
- **Food Industry:** Juice clarification, cheese production, meat tenderization, baking.
- **Detergent Industry:** Biological detergents contain enzymes to remove stains at lower temperatures.
- **Leather Industry:** Enzymes soften animal hides as an eco-friendly alternative to chemicals.
- **Paper Industry:** Enzymes help bleach paper pulp, reducing chemical pollution.
- **Pharmaceutical Industry:** Enzymes are used in diagnostic kits and medicines.

## What Students Should Remember
Listen carefully here: The industrial uses of enzymes frequently appear in long questions. Make sure you can list at least three industries and name the specific enzyme used in each.
